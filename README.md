\# Pandas Sales \& Customer Analytics



\## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing sales and customer data using Python and Pandas. The objective is to clean raw data, perform data analysis, identify business insights, and provide useful business recommendations.



\## Business Problem

The company wants to understand its sales performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, profitability, and salesperson performance.



\## Dataset Description

\- Dataset: Sales \& Customer Analytics

\- Initial Records: 120 rows

\- Columns: 10

\- Dataset Type: Synthetic practice dataset



\## Tools \& Technologies

\- Python

\- Pandas

\- Google Colab

\- Jupyter Notebook



\## Project Workflow



\### Stage 1: Data Understanding

Explored dataset structure, columns, data types, and missing values.



\### Stage 2: Data Cleaning

Handled missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent text, invalid values, and dates.



\### Stage 3: Data Transformation

Created new columns such as TotalSales, DiscountAmount, NetSales, Profit, SalesCategory, and OrderType.



\### Stage 4: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Analysed overall sales, city-wise sales, product performance, and customer performance.



\### Stage 5: Advanced Business Analysis

Performed monthly, category-wise, and salesperson-wise analysis using GroupBy and Pivot Tables.



\### Stage 6: Customer \& Order Performance

Analysed customer frequency, order values, discount patterns, and purchasing behaviour.



\### Stage 7: Advanced Business Insights

Identified repeat customers, product performance, profitability, and salesperson performance.



\### Stage 8: Final Project Summary

Validated the final dataset and prepared business recommendations and conclusions.



\## Key Business Insights

\- Identified top-performing cities and products.

\- Analysed customer purchasing behaviour.

\- Compared category-wise sales and profitability.

\- Evaluated salesperson performance.

\- Analysed discount and profit patterns.



\## Business Recommendations

\- Maintain sufficient stock of best-performing products.

\- Focus on high-performing cities.

\- Introduce loyalty offers for repeat customers.

\- Review pricing and costs of low-profit categories.

\- Share successful sales strategies across the sales team.



\## Conclusion

This project provided practical experience in data cleaning, transformation, exploratory data analysis, and business decision-making using Python and Pandas.



The dataset used in this project is synthetic and created for learning and portfolio demonstration purposes.
